# Problem Understanding

## The Problem
•	What problem is the client experiencing?
    o The business is currently struggling to accurately track its physical products and inventory. It is difficult to know how many products are currently available, how quickly products are being sold, and which products are running low or are out of stock.
•	What is currently happening?
    o The business currently manages product and stock information manually using spreadsheets and other records. As the business grows, maintaining accurate and up-to-date stock information becomes increasingly difficult. Manual processes can also result in outdated or incorrect information.
•	Why is the current process causing problems?
    o The manual process takes staff time and increases the possibility of human error. If stock information is not updated quickly, staff may not know the actual quantity available, which can lead to customers being given incorrect information and delays when purchasing products.

## People Affected
•	Who is affected by this problem?
    o The store itself
    o The staff
    o The customers
•	How are they affected?
    o The store - loses sales over too much time spent on redundant activities
    o The staff - Spend additional time maintaining spreadsheets and manually checking stock levels
    o The customers - May experience delays or receive incorrect information about product availability

## Proposed Solution
•	We propose developing a web-based Inventory Management System that allows staff to manage products and stock information digitally instead of relying on spreadsheets and manual records.
Staff will be able to add, update and remove products, adjust stock quantities, search for products, and view stock history. Customers will be able to view the available products and their current availability.
The system will validate information before it is saved and will restrict inventory management functions to authorized staff members. This will help the business maintain more accurate and up-to-date inventory information while reducing the amount of time staff spend managing stock manually.

## Success Criteria
•	What problems should the system reduce?
    o The system should reduce errors caused by manual stock management, reduce the amount of time staff spend maintaining inventory records, and reduce the possibility of customers receiving incorrect information about product availability.
•	What should become easier?
    o Tracking stock quantities should become easier and faster. Staff should be able to see which products are available, low in stock, or out of stock. They should also be able to view stock history and update inventory information efficiently.
•	What should users be able to do that they cannot easily do now?
    o Staff should be able to manage and update inventory digitally in one system instead of relying on spreadsheets and manual records
    o Customers should be able to view current product information and availability without needing staff to manually check the stock.
