## Client Brief

We are a growing business that sells physical products.

At the moment, we manage information about our products and stock using spreadsheets and other manual records.

As the business grows, we are finding it increasingly difficult to keep our stock information accurate and up to date.

We would like to explore a digital solution that can help us manage our products and keep track of what is happening with our inventory.

We are open to your team's recommendations on how the system should work.
